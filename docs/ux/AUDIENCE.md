# Who it's for

Swarm Studio has two homes and seven audiences. Six of them build, and each gets a **lens**: one app, but its
own tools and its own flow. The seventh browses the Feed and may become a builder.

## Two homes

| Home | Where | What happens there |
|---|---|---|
| **Studio** (private) | `hermes swarm`, the galaxy | You make things. Your builds are the stars. |
| **Feed** (public) | swarm.design-bakery.com, plus the Feed tools inside Hermes | People show off builds and tell their story, try them, remix them, save them to boards and join Jams. |

A finished build travels from the Studio to the Feed (Post to Feed). A Feed card travels back into the Studio
(Remix). That loop is the product.

## Six lenses

A lens decides which stations orbit your build, which room opens first, and which options the Start sheet offers
when you expand it. It never decides what you're *allowed* to do: you can switch lens at any time, and ⌘K
reaches everything.

| Lens | Who | Wants | Doesn't want to see | Stations | Opens on | Main action | Success |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Just make it** | anyone; the default | type an idea, get the whole thing | options, steps, code, tool names | none while it builds: a progress ring on the star | the result card | Build, then Open / Share | a link to show a friend, in one go |
| **Design** | designers | pages, sections, look, images, review, export | code, data, deploy logs, games | Design deck | Design deck | edit a section | a polished set of pages to export or hand off |
| **Develop** | developers | a working app, its code, files, data, checks, deploy, GitHub | marketing, games, narration | Engine room; the design as a reference only | Engine room | change the app by chat, Open app | an app that runs, plus a repo or deploy URL |
| **Game** | game makers | a playable game fast, then tune how it feels | site pages, data, business tools | Game room (+ Film studio for a trailer) | Game room | Play | a playable link (and a trailer) |
| **Mix** | makers between design and web | a designed site that also works as an app | marketing tools, games | Design deck + Engine room | Design deck | switch between look and function | a designed site that works |
| **Launch** | web + marketing | a site plus promo video, launch posts, show card, Feed post | code, data internals | Design deck + Film studio + Launch pad | Launch pad | Share / Post (always confirms first) | launched, with posts ready to send |

The seventh audience, **Feed visitors**, don't build yet. They swipe, try, save and remix. Remix opens the Studio
with the Just make it lens.

For the hackathon there's one more watcher: **the judges**, who see a demo video under 90 s. Just make it, Game
and Design are the lenses that film best.

## How a lens is chosen (proposed, awaiting owner)

You type the idea; the studio guesses the lens from it ("a roguelike where…" → Game) and shows the guess as one
small chip beside Build. Tap the chip to change it, the way Maps offers driving, walking or transit. Nobody is
ever asked "who are you?".

## Open decisions (owner)

1. Guess the lens from the idea, with a chip to change it, or show 6 buttons up front?
2. Is the split right? Mix = design + app; Launch = site + promo + posts.
3. Game: only the game, or game + trailer + Arcade publishing?
4. Scope for 2026-11-11 17:00 EST: build the structure (layers, one input, lens chip, stations per lens, sheets,
   ⌘K) and polish Just make it, Game and Design; the other lenses run over today's tools and grow later?
5. Should the Studio show the Feed as its outermost layer (beyond the galaxy), or as a separate site that Hermes
   links to?
