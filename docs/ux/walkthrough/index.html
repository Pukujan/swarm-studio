Studio walkthrough

The real Studio (main at fd29e7d), screen by screen in the order a newcomer meets it, at 1440 and 390 wide. It ran on its own port with a throwaway data folder: six small projects (a two-version site with a visitor, an app, a game, a film whose footage failed, a site left half built, one in Trash), a model that always fails, and no Hermes. Each picture is checked against docs/ux/RULES.md, PATTERNS.md, REQUIREMENTS.md and the owner's rules. Faults I saw are listed first. Faults marked 'measured' come from scripts/walkthrough-audit.js, which looks for tooltips, targets under 40 px (44 on touch), blue, purple or green chrome, raw errors, internal words and sentences at rest. The steps after the Make box pretend Hermes is on, so the screens a person with Hermes sees are shown. Targets under 40 px were found on every screen (sidebar rows, menu items, chips) and are noted once, on Projects, rather than on each picture. Nothing was fixed.
