# 0006. Art direction

- Date: 2026-11-12
- Status: accepted

## Context

Early builds and samples leaned on one anime look for everything, and Meshy 3D was offered before the person had
asked for it.

## Decision

Relayed by the trailer session on 2026-11-12 *(our wording; the owner's exact words are not on file)*:

- Websites and samples: mostly realistic people or a mature painted look, with anime as one option.
- Game characters and trailers: the anime family.
- No Meshy unless the person chose 3D.

## Consequences

- The default image prompts for sites and samples are not anime.
- A 2D game shows no Meshy and no credits (spec 27: Meshy is opt-in).
