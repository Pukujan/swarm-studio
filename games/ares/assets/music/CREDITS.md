# ARES music

Made for ARES in Google Flow Music (Lyria 3.5) from our own text prompts, in November 2026, then
trimmed and loudness-matched (-17 LUFS). Flow Music embeds Google's SynthID watermark.

| file | moment | Flow title |
| --- | --- | --- |
| title.m4a | title screen, win | Regal Shadows Overture |
| stage.m4a | every stage | Castle Clockwork Stage Theme |
| boss.m4a | bosses and the titan | Final Showdown (Gothic Boss Theme) |
| teknium.m4a | Teknium | Tick-Tock Tempest |
| death.m4a | game over (first 16 s, faded) | Fallen in the Moonlight |
