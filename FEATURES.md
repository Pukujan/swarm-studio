# Swarm Studio: what it does

Every feature works in three places: on the canvas, as a `/swarm …` command, and as an action of the `swarm_studio` / `swarm_design` tools that the Hermes agent calls when you just ask in chat.

## At a glance

One prompt in the Hermes app, and a crew of agents does the work on a canvas you can watch and steer.

| Area | What Swarm does | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **UI / UX design** | UI/UX ideation, layout and copy for every page; a crew of agents (8 by default, up to 24) designing on one canvas at once (collaborative agent design); automated review that finds and fixes problems | works |
| **Prototyping** | Automated prototyping: pages are wired into a clickable prototype you can play on desktop or phone; comment on a section and an agent reworks it live | works |
| **Images** | Pictures painted for every page, with stand-in stock photos while they are made | works |
| **One-click deploy** | A live site on GitHub Pages, Vercel or Cloudflare Pages, on your own account | works |
| **Design to full-stack app** | The design becomes a working app with a server, sign-in, payments and an admin page, deployed to Vercel | works |
| **Image to motion, video** | A promo film from the site's own copy and pictures: stills animated by a video model, voice and music added, one click | works |
| **Game and asset design** | Original games with painted sprites, backdrops, icons, repeating tiles, a UI kit, props and effect animations, scaled to the game's size; the chroma key is measured from the painted picture; the asset library (about 14,600 assetrizz pictures plus packs you import, credits kept) can be searched and picked from by API or asked for by the crew; 3D characters through Meshy | works (library picking has no screen yet) |
| **Game development** | Browser games in three.js, Phaser or PixiJS, written and play-tested by the crew; Godot 4 project when Godot is installed; Unity and Unreal ports as source | works (Unity and Unreal are source only) |
| **Swarm Feed** | Publish a build, let others try it, remix with credit | works |
| **Swarm Jams** | Jam events and hackathons: a theme, a deadline, entries and votes | first version |
| **Collaborative boards, events** | Boards where a team pins notes for the swarm; online and in-person events | next |
| **App stores** | A full-stack app store, deploy to Steam, mobile app stores, more cloud targets | next |

The tables below list each feature with where it lives on the canvas and its command.

## Build

| Feature | What you see | Canvas | Command |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Swarm build** | A planner picks the theme and pages, then 4–24 agents stream sections onto the canvas at the same time. A typical build is 4 pages and about 30 sections in 30–60 s. | Brief box → **Build** | `/swarm <brief>` · `hermes swarm "<brief>" --wait` · `swarm_design` tool |
| **Live agents** | Each agent has a cursor, a name and a colour. You see the tool it picks, a caret where it is typing, a one-line note about what it is doing, and a break room while it waits for work. | Agents panel, Log tab | — |
| **Planner stream** | The theme (colours, fonts, radius) and the page plan as they are written. | **Planner** | — |
| **Wires** | Buttons that link pages become wires between frames. | always on | — |
| **Generated images** | Photos from your Hermes image provider (your Codex login by default). A sketch holds each spot, then the photo fades in. Heroes, places, products and people always get one. While a photo is being made, the studio can show a matching stand-in photo (`GET /api/standin?q=&w=&h=`): first the shipped library (a few hundred Pexels photos, each catalogued by a vision model with a caption, subjects, place, shot, mood, copy space, colours and the kinds of business it suits, hosted on the Octo R2 CDN; rebuild or extend it with `node scripts/stock-library.mjs`, which reads `engine/stock/themes.json`), then a picture an earlier build made, then a live Pexels search if `PEXELS_API_KEY` is in `~/.hermes/.env` (or `PEXELS_OCTO_LEASE`, a working key from Octo's vault that goes through Octo's proxy), then public-domain photos from Openverse. The reviewer's model tags each found photo once and drops anything that isn't a clean photo. Stand-ins are never saved into a build, and each one keeps its credit. Swap any photo in **Design → Pictures** (edit the words, 🎨 New picture), or ➕ Add one to a section. | **Models** | `/swarm images [auto\|off\|<provider> [model]]` |
| **Models** | Choose the models for planning, building, reviewing and images. The builders' model is also right in the chat box, next to Agents (recent picks, or Other… for the full list). | **☰ More → Models**, or **Model** in the chat box | `/swarm models [<role> <model\|off\|reset>]` |
| **Lite mode** | One live page at a time; the others rest as snapshots and wake when you zoom in or an agent works on them. Auto turns it on for machines short on memory, for everyone half a minute after a build finishes, and while you're in Play. | **Models** | `/swarm lite [on\|off\|auto]` |

## Edit

| Feature | What you see | Canvas | Command |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Design panel** | Click any section and change its layout, padding, gap, fill, stroke, radius and type. Variables change every page. Edits are saved with the build. Opens by itself when you select a section and closes after; × closes it. | **☰ More → Design** (D) | — |
| **Comments to agents** | Pin a comment on a section ("make the hero darker"). An agent picks it up, rewrites that section live and says in the chat when it's done. A rewrite that comes back cut off is thrown away and the section stays as it was. Without a section selected, the planner works out which sections you mean. | 💬 tool | `/swarm fix <what to change>` |
| **Undo and redo** | ⌘Z / Ctrl+Z undoes your last change, ⇧⌘Z / Ctrl+Y redoes it: Design edits, your text edits, an agent's rewrite of a section, and putting back a version. Undone changes stay undone when the file reopens. ↶ ↷ at the end of the Design deck's tool dock too. | **↶ ↷**, ⌘Z | `/undo` · `/redo` in the chat |
| **Versions** | Every version a section has had (as built, each review fix, each rewrite from your comments, your text edits), each listed once. Put any one back; ⌘Z undoes that too. | **🕘 Versions** in the Design panel | — |
| **Your tools** | The bottom bar: **Move** (V) selects and pans, **✏️ Text** (T) lets you edit every word of a section yourself, **💬 Comment** (C) asks an agent. Frame, Shape, Image and Connect light up while an agent uses them. | bottom toolbar | — |
| **Visual review** | Every page is checked at desktop and phone width. Measured problems come first: a panel sitting on top of other text, or a half-written tag, found by measuring the page, not guessed. Then a vision model looks at both screenshots and names what a visitor would notice. Agents fix it all live, one agent per section. | **🔎 Review** | `/swarm review [n]` |

## Show

| Feature | What you see | Canvas | Command |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Clickable prototype** | Every page, with working links, on desktop or mobile. A layout guard moves any text panel that a builder floated over a map or picture beside it if it would cover other text at that width (exports and published sites get it too). | **▶ Play** (P) | `/swarm play` |
| **Motion** | Sections rise in as they scroll into view and buttons lift on hover. Bold or playful themes move faster and springier. | **✨ Motion** (M) | `/swarm motion [on\|off]` |
| **Watch a visitor** | 3–4 people with different goals use the site. A cursor acts each one out, with a thought bubble. | **👀 Watch a visitor** (V) | — |
| **Files** | Every build is a file with its metadata: brief, dates, pages, theme colours and fonts, models, pictures, reviews, edits, live links, video. Name it, tag it, file it in a folder, add a note (⋯ → Details). Search every field (`tag:`, `folder:`, `on:vercel`, `has:video`), sort by newest, oldest, name or size, group by day or folder; unfinished runs fold away. | **Files** tab | `/swarm files [words]`, `/swarm info [n]` |
| **Folders** | Make folders (＋📁), drag a build onto a folder to file it, drag builds to put them in your own order (sort: My order), drag folder headings to reorder folders, ⋯ on a folder to rename or delete it (its builds stay). Empty folders stay put. | **Files** tab, By folder | `/swarm folders` · `/swarm folder <n> <name\|none>` · `/folder <name>` in the chat |
| **Traces** | Every model call is recorded with its build: which agent, page and section, the instructions and request it was sent, the raw answer, time to first text, tokens, and any error. Open it per build (⋯ → Traces) or per section (🔍 in the Design panel); filter by section, agent or kind (build, fix, review…). Recording is on per build and can be turned off (⋯ → Record traces). Hermes traces its own agent loop; these are the swarm's calls, which go straight from the canvas server. With `HERMES_LANGFUSE_PUBLIC_KEY` / `_SECRET_KEY` set they also go to Langfuse, next to the Hermes traces. | **⋯ → 🔍 Traces**, **🔍 Trace of this section** | `/swarm trace [n] [words]` · `/swarm trace call <id>` · `/swarm trace on\|off` · `/trace` in the chat |
| **Promo video** | The planner writes a storyboard and a voiceover from the site's own copy and photos and casts the voices and music for the theme. Your idea leads ("a plane flying to Hawaii" puts Hawaii on screen), and new pictures are painted for what it asks to see. Plans: Promo, Kinetic type, Slideshow, Teaser, Customer story; cuts: colour wipe, fade, zoom, slide, flash; plus your own idea. Effects over the whole video (sparkle, stars, fireworks, breeze, confetti, snow) come from the storyboard and from the words in your idea ("sparkly with fireworks" always gets both). HyperFrames renders the MP4 in the site's theme; Kokoro (local, no account) reads it over a music bed ducked under the voice. Saved where you can see it, in `~/Movies/Swarm Studio/<build> · <date>/`, every cut kept as v1, v2…, and the Video view lists them all to switch between. Download, Show in Finder, ⛶ Big screen. Any OpenAI-compatible TTS (`SWARM_TTS_URL`) can replace Kokoro. | **🎬 Video**, or `/video [style] [idea]` in the chat | `/swarm video [n] [style] [cut] [voice\|off] [nomusic] [idea]` |
| **Chat commands** | Type `/` in the canvas chat for a list: /play, /review, /fix, /video, /export, /publish, /motion, /files, /open, /info, /trace, /folder, /models, /app, /game, /canvas, /new, /help. Anything else starts a new build. | Chat box | same as `/swarm …` |
| **Tidy workspace** | Play and Publish stay in the top bar; Review, Video, Export, Models and the panels live in **☰ More**. ⇤ shrinks the side panel to a small chat over the canvas (the chat always stays); ⇥ brings it back. | **☰ More**, **⇤** | — |

## Ship

| Feature | What you get | Canvas | Command |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Export** | Static HTML pages, a React + Tailwind project (Vite, one component per section, builds as is) and an ABA handoff (`spec.md` + `blueprint.json`), plus a zip, copied to `~/Documents/Swarm Studio/<build> · <date>/` as v1, v2…. Motion is included. | **⬇ Export** | `/swarm export [n]` |
| **Publish** | A live website, wherever you host. **GitHub Pages** uses your `gh` login: one public repo per site, a `you.github.io/site-name/` link in about a minute. **Vercel** and **Cloudflare Pages** go to your own account: paste an access token once (like Dyad does), or an Octo vault grant so the token never leaves your vault, and get a `…vercel.app` or `…pages.dev` link. **This computer** hosts it at `127.0.0.1:4319/site/<build>/` straight away, and is the fallback if a host fails. Publishing again updates the same site; projects it didn't make are never touched. | **🌐 Publish** | `/swarm publish [n] [github\|vercel\|cloudflare\|local]` · `/swarm hosts` |
| **Working app** | The design becomes a full-stack app with dyad's engine: React + shadcn/ui on Vite, Nitro server routes, forms that validate and save, an `/admin` table. A site with a promo game also gets **Rewards**: points from bookings and purchases and from the game's win screen, a `/rewards` page with the balance and history, and a shop of perks from made-up partners (never a real company's name) that the points buy. The **App** tab shows each step, a live preview (desktop, tablet, phone) and the code with its git history; type a change in the chat and the engine makes it. Loaded only when you first open the App tab. | **App** tab | `/swarm app [n] [change]` · `/app` in the chat |
| **App kit** | In the Studio, an app is planned before it is built: who uses it, a map of its screens with arrows, the things it keeps, what it connects to (sign-in, payments, email, files, any API you name, each Ready, Needs a key or Not chosen), and where to start. "Do I need a flights API?" gets an answer and a choice in the chat, not a build. Lock writes it into the spec; the app build makes exactly those screens, data and connections, then checks the app's code against them (engine/appkit.mjs, web/kit-app.js) | works |
| **Deck** | 16:9 slides from the brand and the story, like a slide maker that already knows the project: an outline (from the build's story when there is one), every slide drawn small on the board, 25 looks from html-ppt, and where to start. Present plays it full screen with the usual keys; HTML is one file that presents itself; PDF is one page a slide. No PowerPoint file (engine/deck.mjs, web/kit-deck.js) | works |
| **Game mode** | 🎮 asks a few questions one at a time (chips or your own words, "Just build it" any time), writes an original spec you can edit, then builds a playable browser game (three.js, Phaser or PixiJS, everything made in code, sound in WebAudio) in the App tab. "Similar to" sets only the feel. After the build the studio plays the game for a few seconds in a headless browser (presses start, then taps, Space and the arrows); a run that ends at once, a crash or a missing canvas gets one fix turn and a second try. A new game also publishes its state for the test (`window.__swarm`: the scene, the hero's position and hitbox, the enemies and companions), and the test holds each control the spec names and checks the move happened (right, left, jump, crouch standing still, slide at a run), that an enemy does not pace on the spot and that there is one companion; what fails goes back to the crew as exact asks, and a game that plays differently when it is being tested is sent back too (`engine/playcheck.mjs`). A promo game links back to its site only when that site is online (the spec card offers **Put <site> online first**: a connected Vercel or Cloudflare, else GitHub Pages), with the run's score as points to claim (`?game=<slug>&points=N`), and a web address in the code that the studio did not give it (a made-up domain is someone else's) is sent back to be removed. **How big?** picks the size: **Mini game** (the default, one engine call) or **Big game**, a showcase action roguelike whose spec has a hub, a chain of rooms with 3+ room types, 2–3 weapons, choose-one-of-three boons from patrons, 5+ enemy types, a two-phase boss, people who talk with portraits and a run summary. A big game's art (title picture, portraits, boon icons, room backdrops with no figures in them, and sprites of the hero, each enemy and the boss, painted on a flat background and cut out to transparent PNGs) is painted first by the same art director as a site's pictures, in the project's drawn style, into the app's `public/art/`; the fighters stay upright and flip left or right rather than rotating; then it builds in five stages (core fight, rooms, weapons and boons, hub and dialogue, boss and summary), each with its own turns, the typecheck and build gates, a play test and a fix turn. | **🎮 Game** | `/swarm game [idea]` · `/swarm game build [idea]` · `/game` in the chat |
| **⚡ Auto mode** | One prompt runs the whole studio on one timeline: the site's design, a review of every page, a promo video led by your prompt, the working app, a 🎮 Play page with three original mini games (a tiny roguelike, a reflex game, a puzzle) and a shared leaderboard, a $2 pack of credits the games spend, the data in Supabase, and a deploy to Vercel. The studio follows along (canvas, then Video, then App) with a timeline panel and its clock in the corner. The checkout is a demo, labelled as one: a fake test card and a `demo_txn_…` receipt, no money moves; with a Stripe test key (`STRIPE_SECRET_KEY=sk_test_…`) it uses Stripe test mode instead. For Supabase, press **🔌 Connect Supabase** in the Auto window: Hermes's own Supabase connector signs you in in the browser (no keys to copy), and Auto reuses or makes a free project called `swarm-studio` with its `swarm_records` table. It never makes a paid one. Keys still work instead: `SUPABASE_URL` and `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` in `~/.hermes/.env`. If a late step fails, what was built still deploys. | **⚡ Auto** | `/swarm auto <prompt>` · `/swarm auto status\|stop` |
| **App deploy** | The app, server routes included, on your Vercel account. | **🚀 Deploy** in the App tab | `/swarm deploy [n]` |
| **Real app handoff** | `aba/spec.md` in the export is written for an app builder such as ABA (the author's own), which turns the design into a full-stack app. | in the export | `/swarm export` |
| **Search & share** | Every page ships its own search title and description, a 1200×630 share picture picked with no one asked (the page's hero, the key art or a shot of the page, plus a square; one click in Publish swaps it, and the Feed poster uses the same one), its own favicon and touch icon (apps and games too, never Dyad's) and JSON-LD that only says what the page says (no made-up ratings, prices or addresses), and every site `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml` and a 404 page, corrected with the real address at publish. The page planner writes them, a reviewer checks length, duplicates, one h1 and alt text; ask "improve the SEO" or "target people searching for X" and the crew rewrites them. Older builds get it by exporting again (`node scripts/seo.mjs`). | **Design** panel, nothing selected | `/swarm seo [n] [what]` |
| **Story** | Every build gets a story: five to eight beats (a hook, what it is, who it is for, its best moments, how it was made, where to go next) written from its brand and its pages, never from what you told the crew, with nothing "live" until it is. Each beat is a card in the build's own colours and type, with its real pictures and screens, drawn for a phone, a desktop and a square. The type (Launch, About me, Walkthrough, Trailer, Event, How it was made) is picked from what it is. | API now (`/api/runs/:id/story`); the Overview lane next | — |
| **Files** | Every build is saved, can be reopened, and replays exactly. | Files tab | `/swarm files` · `/swarm open <n>` |
| **Swarm Feed** | Share builds at [swarm.design-bakery.com](https://swarm.design-bakery.com): **connect** once (a code you approve in your browser; a guest there becomes a member with 100 credits), **post** a published build with a poster, its pages, its crew and its files (optionally into a jam), **remix** anyone's build (its prompt starts a new run; posting it credits the original), and **login** signs your browser in as you. | — | `/swarm connect` · `/swarm post [n] [--jam <id>] [caption]` · `/swarm remix <b_id> [change]` · `/swarm login` |
